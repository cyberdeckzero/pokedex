# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pokémon fans browsing the national catalogue, comparing base stats, and assembling teams of up to six. The existing Angular assessment also needs clear, inspectable state and recovery behavior.

## Product Purpose

A working digital Pokédex with the recognizable character of the original handheld device. Browsing, inspecting an entry, and saving a team remain the primary jobs.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, curious, precise. A friendly field instrument from the Pokémon world rather than a generic administration dashboard.

## Anti-references

Generic white dashboard cards, unrelated neon science-fiction effects, decorative controls that do nothing, animation that delays access to data, and a marketing landing page in front of the application.

## Design Principles

- Make the physical Pokédex recognizable through its red enclosure, lens, hinge, and LCD displays.
- Give every visible control a real action; keep purely physical details decorative and hidden from assistive technology.
- Use modern motion to explain selection and interaction, with data visible throughout.
- Keep all assessment behavior, including table sorting/pagination, keyboard autocomplete, optimistic teams, and recoverable errors.
- Preserve the Angular architecture and keep added dependencies modest.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep visible focus, semantic buttons and table headers, labels, status feedback, Escape/focus handling, reduced-motion support, and horizontal table scrolling. Use readable text on LCD backgrounds and preserve keyboard access to every action.
